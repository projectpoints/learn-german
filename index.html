A1 Level – 56 Hours

  • Introduce yourself, alphabet & numbers

  • Daily schedule, appointments, seasons & weather
  • Basic sentence connectives & negation

A2 Level – 56 Hours

  • Past events, perfect tense, professions & CV
  • Shopping, money, subjunctive II (wishes)
  • Countries, travel planning, healthy nutrition

B1 Level – 66 Hours

  • Smalltalk, languages, success/failures
  • Past tense, modal verbs, two-part connectives

  • Relative clauses, declination of adjectives

B2 Level – 66 Hours

  • Complaint letters, e-mails & telephone etiquette
  • Advanced passive, n-declination, causal indicators
  • Indirect speech, modal particles, nominalization
  • Presentation & discussion skills
